about me

I'm Meeta, an AI/ML engineer doing my M.Sc. in Web Engineering at TU Chemnitz. I like taking an idea all the way from raw data to something people can actually use.

Before my master's I spent almost a year and a half at Goldenbird Automations, building data pipelines and deploying computer-vision and LLM services for smart-automation products. Since then I've been building my own systems, mostly around GenAI and agents: Guardian, a retraining agent for jet-engine models; RouteLens, an auditor for AI explanations; and Atlas, a chat agent over nine years of German accident data.

Things I got wrong

The times my first answer was wrong, and what I did about it.

RouteLens · XAI evaluation

"Attention explanations never flip."

what happenedTrue on 5 prompts. At 150 seeded runs attention flipped on 70–86% of prompts. I retracted it and kept the finding that replicated.

RouteLens · second retraction

"LIME is the most convincing explainer but the least faithful."

what happenedIt looked true on 5 prompts but didn't replicate at 150 runs, so I retracted it. My own Router-Attribution Agreement metric also came out at roughly zero, and I reported that null result.

Guardian · data augmentation

"Synthetic failure data will help both models."

what happenedIt cut LSTM error by 29%. It did not help the GBM at any of 21 configs. I reported both.

Atlas · prompt design

"More safety instructions make the agent safer."

what happenedA small local model stopped calling tools entirely. An A/B test traced it to prompt length, so smaller models now get a shorter instruction set.

10-K RAG · ingestion

"Re-running a partition is safe."

what happenedA row-count check caught 270 chunks where 135 were expected. Deterministic UUID5 IDs fixed the duplicates.

Guardian · evaluation

"The test scores are clean."

what happenedI found a label clip leaking into the test set, normalization fit on validation data, and an unscaled LSTM that collapsed to RMSE 41.6. All fixed.
